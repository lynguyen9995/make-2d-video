# Toon Demo — story bible
Reference project for the toon cast (`engine/toon.js`, `engine/toon3d.js`). No voiceover: it only shows the API.
- Cast: Nhóc (orange tee with "?", the channel kid), ông đồ (scholar), a worried banker, a biker uncle, two caricatures of public figures.
- Sets: classroom, office 1995, sunburst split screen, keynote stage, 3D chip on a night cliff.
- Style: `paper` (neutral grade). Comic pop text uses Nunito 900 italic; handwriting uses Patrick Hand.
