# Toon catalog
Render with `node render.mjs --project examples/toon-catalog --sheet` and open the stills: it shows EVERYTHING the toon cast
already has (front poses, side cycles, expressions, cast, props, sets). Check it before drawing anything new, reuse what exists,
and after adding a new asset to toon.js / toon-rig.js / toon-props.js add it to the matching catalog shot here.
